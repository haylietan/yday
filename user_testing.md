## Patterns Among Users
I noticed the majority of the users did not understand what the "Dial" tab was for. There was also some confusion with the "2s take" tab as well. The older testers (above 50) were very confused on the app and did not know where to start at all. The other 3 testers (in their 20s), were able to easily navigate the app and figure out how to complete the tasks. This is largely because the app is targeted towards Gen z/the younger audience. One of the users mentioned it felt intuitive since it resembles the structure of other social media apps like Tiktok.

Another observation I made was that successfully completing a task does not necessarily equate to the user understanding what was going on. For instance, Hank completed all three tasks but that was mainly due to him clicking around until he found the right feature. On top of this, several users struggled to understand the overall purpose of the app during the five-second test. This tells me that although the app's functionality is there, its purpose and core loop are not clear. I also noticed redundant feedback regarding the inability to retake videos and the strict two-second recording limit. This made me reconsider whether these constraints improve the experience or makes it unnecessarily strict.

## What I Would Change
I will definitel workshop the "Dial" term and perhaps switch it out for "Connect" or "Share" to make it more intuitive for users. I also want to play around a bit more with the UI. Kaitlyn mentioned that due to the low contrast with the color scheme, it was hard to find certain features like the recording vlog button. I want to make the user experience a lot more straightforward. Removing excess features and text and maybe even dropping one of the tabs (2s take or Dial).
